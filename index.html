PROJECT FINDINGS

Key Findings

The project report highlights several common challenges and patterns related to digital technology use among senior citizens.

📱

Limited Device Usage

Some senior citizens use smartphones regularly, while others use digital devices only occasionally or rarely.

😟

Fear and Anxiety

Fear of making mistakes, scams and unfamiliar technology can reduce confidence.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Assistance

Many senior citizens depend on children or grandchildren for help with digital tasks.

⚙️

Complex Interfaces

Small text, complicated menus and unfamiliar applications can create difficulties.

💬

Communication

Communication with family and friends is one of the important uses of digital technology.

💰

Digital Transactions

Some senior citizens are comfortable with digital payments, while others remain hesitant.

🎓

Training Needs

Hands-on workshops and simple explanations can support digital learning.

🛡️

Safety Awareness

Awareness about fraud, suspicious messages and protection of personal information is important.

STAY SAFE ONLINE

Simple Rules for Digital Safety

Staying safe online is an important part of digital literacy.

🔒 Never share your OTP with anyone.
💳 Never share your UPI PIN.
🔗 Avoid clicking unknown or suspicious links.
🔑 Keep passwords private and secure.
📞 Verify unexpected calls and messages before taking action.

CONCLUSION

Building a More Inclusive Digital World

Digital literacy has become an essential part of everyday life for senior citizens. It enables them to stay connected with family and friends and access important services such as banking, healthcare, shopping and e-governance.

However, senior citizens may face challenges such as limited knowledge, lack of confidence, physical difficulties and fear of making mistakes while using digital technologies.

Patient guidance, simple training materials and continued support from family, community organisations, NGOs and government initiatives can help reduce these barriers.

Digital literacy can therefore contribute to greater independence, confidence, dignity and social inclusion among senior citizens.
